ACME Vendor Agreement

1. Services
Vendor will provide document processing and workflow automation services to ACME.

2. Fees
ACME will pay Vendor according to the applicable order form.

3. Limitation of Liability
Vendor liability is capped at fees paid by ACME in the previous 12 months.

4. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not cured within 30 days.
