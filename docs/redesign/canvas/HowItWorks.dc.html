Questions, answered

What people usually want to know before uploading a contract.

Is this legal advice?

No. MaSign is an AI-assisted review tool that helps you read a contract. It does not replace a lawyer and does not make legal decisions.

Which files can I upload?

PDF, DOCX and TXT. MaSign reads the text and splits it into passages, so every result can point back to where it came from.

Where does my contract go?

The text is stored as passages in the database of the instance you use. To answer a question or run a review, only the relevant passages are sent to the configured AI provider (Anthropic or OpenAI). There is no analytics or tracking script.

Whose side does the review take?

The customer’s — the party receiving the services, goods or licence — unless the contract makes clear you are the other party.

What if the document isn’t a contract?

MaSign labels every upload as a contract, uncertain, or not a contract. A clean review of an invoice says the rubric may not apply, rather than “nothing found”.

What if a contract contains instructions to the AI?

A guardrail checks each passage before it is sent. Sentences aimed at the AI are removed, and the workspace tells you a passage was withheld.
