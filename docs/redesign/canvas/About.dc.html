Before you rely on it

Clear expectations

MaSign gives you a fast first read with sources you can open. Here is what it is — and what it is not.

Read the full disclaimer
  • 01

    Assists, not legal advice

    MaSign supports your review of a contract; it does not replace a lawyer and does not make legal decisions.

  • 02

    AI findings may be imperfect

    Answers and risk findings are generated by a model reading the uploaded text — verify anything that matters against the source passage each one links to.

  • 03

    How your data is used

    Passages relevant to a question or review are sent to the configured AI provider (Anthropic or OpenAI) to generate the answer or finding.
