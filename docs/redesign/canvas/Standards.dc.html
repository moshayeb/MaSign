Comparison rules

Your company standards

MaSign measures every contract against these rules. A term outside them shows up as a deviation in “Before you sign”.

Terms

Payment deadline

Default

The shortest time you accept to pay an invoice.

MaSign default: net 30 days or longer · compared with the Payment deadline key term

Notice period

Default

The longest notice you accept before you can leave or stop a renewal.

MaSign default: at most 60 days · compared with the Notice period key term

Late-payment interest

Default

The highest interest a supplier may charge on a late invoice.

MaSign default: at most 1% per month (12% per year) · compared with the Late-payment interest key term

Termination cost

Changed from default

What you accept paying if you end the contract before its term.

Accept a termination cost of No fee Percent cap Amount cap % of the remaining fees, at most

MaSign default: no early-termination fee · compared with the Termination cost key term. An amount cap also asks for a currency (USD, EUR or SEK)
